# 0013 — Character policy: public domain, historical, original only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) §1.2 invariant 14 forbids brand and franchise characters and permits public-domain characters, historical figures, and original designs. [TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md](../TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) §22 records the decision.

## Options considered

- **Franchise or brand characters** — use recognisable protected characters. Rejected because it creates legal exposure.
- **Public domain, historical, and original designs** — the permitted set.

Source: [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) §1.2; [TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md](../TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) §22.

## Decision

Only public-domain characters, historical figures, and original designs are used.

## Consequences

### Positive

The product avoids trademark and copyright exposure, and original characters become an owned asset.

### Negative

Less instant recognition for learners, and original character design adds art and writing work.

### Revisit if

A licensed character agreement is signed, with the licence recorded in the asset licence record.
