# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual identity or orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not share personal information, learner material, transcripts, or credentials.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized content or attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository security reporting flow. Project maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
